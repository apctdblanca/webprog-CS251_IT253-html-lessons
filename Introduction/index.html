About Me

Hi, I'm Tristan Gabriel Blanca—though most people just call me Tan.

I'm a Computer Science student at Asia Pacific College (APC), currently serving as the Director of External Relations for the Junior Philippine Computer Society (JPCS-APC) and a Notion Campus Leader. While I enjoy collaborating on impactful campus projects like Kalakalan and organizing community tech events, I prefer keeping things laid-back over diving into intense technical problem-solving.

In my downtime, I'm all about chilling and hanging out with friends. You'll usually find me managing multiplayer game servers with my crew, reading manga, building Legos, or just unwinding to a good OPM playlist. I try to bring a lighthearted, easygoing energy to everything I do.
